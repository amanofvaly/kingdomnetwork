## Scope and mode

Homepage storefront (`client/src/App.jsx`), Persuade through immediate marketplace use rather than a marketing-only landing page.

## Audience, job, and action

Individuals begin by searching and comparing church-issued offerings. They may open an offering or its issuing church and must see price, type, issuer, verification, and requirements quickly. Churches should recognize a credible distribution and credential-operations platform and choose to list their ministry.

## Proof, content, and constraints

Use clearly labeled demonstration offerings until production catalog data exists. Show composable pathways, issuing-church identity, verification status, and the Digital Minister Passport. Never imply that an invitation letter guarantees a visa decision.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

A continuous marketplace in warm paper, ministry green, brass, and clay. Search shares the first viewport with a priced pathway whose three requirements are visible; the product catalog begins immediately below.

## Unresolved decisions

Real product data, final prices, partner church identities, brand mark, photography, and production navigation destinations remain open.
