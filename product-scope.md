a dual-sided digital platform connecting international ministries (launching in East Africa) with global supporters, sponsors, and credential-seeking ministers.

1. Core Platform Architecture & User Personas
The platform operates as a multi-tenant hub with two distinct primary user classes:

* Persona A: Individual Users / Ministers

   * Key Objectives: Consume church media/devotionals, donate via local mobile money/cards, apply for biblical certifications/ordinations, request church affiliations, and obtain official visa invitation letters.

   * Core Feature: Digital Minister Passport, A centralized mobile credential wallet displaying active church affiliations, verified ordination statuses, digital certificates earned, and issued visa invitation documents.

* Persona B: Church Administrators & Ministries

   * Key Objectives: Publish media/materials, run project & individual sponsorship campaigns, issue certifications/ordinations, generate visa invitation letters, and manage mobile money/card payouts.

2. Financial Infrastructure & Monetization Engine

* Dynamic Payment Rails:

   * Phase 1 (East Africa Launch): Native integration with M-Pesa, regional East African Mobile Money, and credit/debit cards.

   * Multi-Region Expansion: System architecture must dynamically detect user geolocation/IP and render local payment rails applicable to their region.

* Monetization & Fees:

   * Transaction Fee: Automated platform commission percentage deducted on all incoming donations, sponsorships, and paid certification/letter fees before payout.

   * Tiered Membership System: Database schema must accommodate future premium church subscription tiers for advanced analytics and features.

3. Verification, Trust & Visa Invitation Protocol
To prevent fraud and maintain cross-border compliance, implement a Tiered Trust Engine:

* Tier 1 (Unverified Ministry):

   * Permissions: Can publish free content and accept local low-threshold donations.

   * Restrictions: Cannot launch international sponsorship campaigns or issue official visa invitation letters.

* Tier 2 (Verified Partner Ministry):

   * Requirements: Submission and central admin approval of government registration (NGO/Non-Profit status) and national church fellowship credentials.

   * Permissions: Full access to cross-border payouts, international child/project sponsorships, hosting certification modules, and direct issuance of visa invitation letters.

* Milestone-Based Sponsorship Escrow:

   * Sponsorship funds (child/project) accumulate in an escrow ledger and release to church mobile wallets upon uploading required proof (photo/video report updates).

4. Content Delivery & Certification Engine

* Adaptive Media Delivery:

   * Low-Bandwidth Priority: Audio-first MP3 streaming, compressed PDFs, and offline caching for regions with limited internet connectivity.

   * Live Stream Capable: HLS video streaming integration for high-bandwidth events.

* Biblical Certification & Ordination System:

   * Interactive Learning: Native support for video/audio course modules with multiple-choice quiz validation.

   * Document Issuance: Capability for churches to manually evaluate offline credentials and issue digital certificates/ordination seals directly to the student’s Digital Minister Passport.
