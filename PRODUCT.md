# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Individuals and ministers seeking church-issued learning, credentials, ordination pathways, affiliations, and invitation letters.
- Churches, bishops, and ministry administrators seeking to publish offerings, issue documents, receive payments, manage supporters, and reach an international audience. Individuals lead the homepage's first commerce story, but churches must receive an equally intentional acquisition path.

## Product Purpose

Kingdom Network is a two-sided marketplace connecting people with church-issued courses, credentials, ordination pathways, affiliations, and invitation documents, while giving churches the infrastructure to publish, sell, verify, and issue those offerings. It launches with an East African operating focus and supports global participation.

## Positioning

Unlike a flat course catalog, Kingdom Network supports composable ministry pathways: courses and prerequisite credentials can combine toward higher titles or final church-issued certificates, which the platform generates and stores in a Digital Minister Passport.

## Operating Context

Individuals may discover an outcome first and then compare issuing churches, discover a church first and then browse its offerings, or buy a direct church offering. Churches manage courses, documents, applications, evaluations, issuance, donations, sponsorships, and payouts. Phase-one payments prioritize M-Pesa, regional mobile money, and cards.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products include standalone certificates, ordination pathways, courses, affiliations, and church-issued invitation letters.
- Some credentials require courses, prior titles, offline credential review, or combinations of multiple certificates.
- The platform generates issued certificates and stores credentials and invitation documents in a Digital Minister Passport.
- Tier 1 ministries may publish free content and accept limited local donations. Only centrally approved Tier 2 partner ministries may issue invitation letters, host certification modules, use cross-border payouts, and run international sponsorships.
- Invitation letters are church-issued supporting documents and must never be presented as guaranteeing visa approval.
- Low-bandwidth access prioritizes audio, compressed documents, and offline-friendly delivery.
- Subscription tiers for churches and location-aware payment rails are future-ready requirements.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Kingdom Network; “Kingdom” may be used where natural in interface language. The voice must be direct, dignified, trustworthy, and commerce-clear. Udemy and Coursera are structural references for marketplace clarity, not visual brands to imitate.

## Product Principles

- Make the purchasable outcome and its issuer understandable within seconds.
- Let people browse by outcome, learning path, or church without forcing one hierarchy.
- Give churches a serious growth and operations proposition, not a secondary footer pitch.
- Make prerequisites, issuing authority, verification state, and document limitations explicit.
- Design for trust and low-bandwidth access from the start.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience should meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations and remain usable on small screens, slower connections, and with reduced motion.
